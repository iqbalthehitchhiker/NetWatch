NetWatch
Learn to monitor, investigate, and diagnose networks — not to build them.
Packet Tracer teaches you to build and configure a network. NetWatch teaches you to watch one, catch problems as they happen, and diagnose the root cause from evidence — telemetry, topology state, and captured packets.
