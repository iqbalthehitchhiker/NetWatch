Every signal is evidence

Five views. One incident. Your job is to connect them.

Topology

See which devices are connected and where the problem could originate. Link colour shifts with latency and loss.

Metrics

Track latency, packet loss, CPU, jitter, and throughput. Anomalies appear as the incident progresses.

Alerts

A timestamped event timeline fires as the incident unfolds. Critical and warning events are colour-coded by severity.

Packets

Inspect simulated captures. Flagged rows highlight anomalous traffic — protocol, direction, and info fields are all clues.

Logs

Timestamped device events let you reconstruct what happened in what order — and where it started.

Two stages of learning

Start by learning to read the signals. Then prove you can diagnose from them.

Stage 1
Teach Mode

Learn while you investigate.

Explanations are available at every step. No scoring, no login.

  • Click any device, metric, alert, or packet row to open a plain-language explanation
  • Understand what each signal means and what it indicates about network health
  • Self-check questions confirm comprehension — ungraded, no attempt limit
  • No root-cause diagnosis required — the payoff is understanding
  • No login needed
then
Stage 2
Quiz Mode

Diagnose without the training wheels.

Explanations hidden. Investigate the evidence, identify the root cause.

  • Explanations are hidden — no Explain Panel, no guided hints
  • Investigate topology, metrics, alerts, packets, and logs yourself
  • Submit a root-cause diagnosis from the multiple-choice options
  • Attempt-limited per scenario — results are recorded server-side
  • Requires student login
